# Customer care and escalation guidelines

## Repeated support contacts
A customer with 3 or more support calls in 90 days is flagged as a repeat contact. Repeat contacts must be handled by a senior agent who reviews the full history before offering anything.

## Escalation path
Escalate to a team lead when: the customer asks for a discount above 20%, the customer has complained to the regulator, or the same issue has been reported three times without a fix. Escalate network problems to the network quality team through a network investigation ticket.

## Tone and commitments
Agents must not promise outcomes they cannot guarantee, such as a network fix date. Every retention conversation ends with a written summary sent to the customer.

## Payment problems
Customers paying by invoice have more failed payments. Offer to switch to direct debit; this is not a retention offer and has no limit.
