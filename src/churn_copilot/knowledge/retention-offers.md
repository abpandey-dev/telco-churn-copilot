# Retention offers policy

## Who is eligible
Retention offers are only for customers with a model churn probability of 0.5 or higher (high risk) or 0.25-0.5 (medium risk). Low-risk customers receive no proactive offer. A customer can receive at most one retention offer every 12 months.

## Offers for medium-risk customers
Medium-risk customers may be offered one of: 10% off the monthly charge for 6 months, or a free data add-on of 10 GB per month for 3 months. Agents should prefer the data add-on for customers on the Basic 10GB plan.

## Offers for high-risk customers
High-risk customers may be offered 20% off the monthly charge for 12 months if they move to a 12-month or 24-month contract. If the customer refuses a contract, the maximum is 10% off for 6 months. Discounts above 20% need approval from a retention team lead.

## Offers when network quality is the driver
If the main churn driver is network quality (dropped-call rate above 2%), do not lead with a discount. First open a network investigation ticket and offer the network-quality compensation described in the network compensation policy. A discount alone does not fix the reason the customer is unhappy.

## Family bundle upsell
Customers without a family bundle may be offered to add a family member at 50% off the second line for 12 months. Family-bundle customers churn markedly less, so this offer is preferred for customers aged 25-55.
