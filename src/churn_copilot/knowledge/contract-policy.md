# Contract and pricing policy

## Contract types
Customers can choose month-to-month (one month notice), 12-month or 24-month contracts. Longer contracts come with a lower monthly price: 12-month contracts are 10% cheaper and 24-month contracts 15% cheaper than month-to-month.

## Moving to a longer contract
A month-to-month customer can move to a 12-month or 24-month contract at any time with no fee. The new price applies from the next billing cycle. Moving to a longer contract is the most effective single retention action for month-to-month customers.

## Early termination
Customers on 12-month or 24-month contracts who leave early pay the remaining monthly charges, capped at 3 months. The fee is waived if the customer moves abroad or qualifies under the network compensation policy (dropped-call rate above 4%).

## Plans
Plans are Basic 10GB (about €15/month), Plus 30GB (about €30/month) and Unlimited 5G (about €50/month). Downgrading a plan is always allowed and is not a retention offer.
