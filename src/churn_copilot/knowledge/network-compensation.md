# Network quality compensation policy

## When compensation applies
A customer is eligible for network-quality compensation if their dropped-call rate has been above 2% for the last 30 days, or if they report repeated coverage problems at their home address that are confirmed by a network investigation ticket.

## Compensation amounts
Eligible customers receive a one-off credit of €10 on their next bill. If the dropped-call rate is above 4%, the credit is €20 and the customer may also leave a month-to-month contract with no notice period.

## Network investigation ticket
Every compensation case must have a network investigation ticket so engineering can fix the cause. Tickets should include the customer's city, the measured dropped-call rate and the time period. Known weak areas are reviewed weekly by the radio planning team.

## What not to do
Do not promise a fix date for network problems. Do not offer compensation more than twice in 12 months for the same address; escalate to the network quality team instead.
