# Data protection (GDPR) and consent for retention outreach

## Legal basis
Churn predictions may be used to improve service for existing customers. Proactive marketing calls or messages with retention offers need the customer's marketing consent. Without consent, offers can only be made when the customer contacts us.

## Automated decisions
Under GDPR Article 22, a churn score must not be the only basis for a decision that significantly affects the customer, such as refusing a contract. A human agent reviews every retention offer, and customers can ask why they were contacted.

## Data minimisation
Retention agents see only the data they need: contract, plan, tenure, charges, support history and network quality. Age and city must not be used to decide offer amounts.

## Retention period
Churn scores are stored for at most 12 months and are deleted when a customer leaves, except where needed for legal obligations.
